La Team Panda

World of Warcraft addons made by pandaren for adventurers.

Feet of Azeroth

Feet of Azeroth is your little travel companion in Azeroth. It displays your current coordinates in real time and tracks the distance you travel through the world of Warcraft, turning every journey into a true exploration trail.

version: 0.8.1
compatibility: classic
download: CurseForge
repository: GitHub

Gasp of Pandaria

Gasp of Pandaria is a light and relaxing mini-game inspired by the aesthetics of Pandaria. It features puzzle grids across three difficulty levels, perfect for quick and relaxing sessions between quests, dungeons, or raids.

version: 0.13.2
compatibility: classic, retail
download: CurseForge
repository: GitHub

Traveler's Notebook

When I play World of Warcraft, I always keep a notebook nearby to write down useful information: NPC locations, ideas, landmarks, or quests I don't want to forget. Traveler's Notebook brings this habit directly into the game, giving you a true adventure journal right at your fingertips.

version: 0.8.1
compatibility: classic, retail
download: CurseForge
repository: GitHub

Wisdom of Azeroth

Wisdom of Azeroth is a lightweight addon that displays an Azeroth-inspired proverb each time you log in. A touch of pandaren wisdom to start your gaming session with a smile.

version: 0.0.3
compatibility: classic
download: CurseForge
repository: GitHub
