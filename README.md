# La Team Panda

See more: [https://lateampanda.github.io](https://lateampanda.github.io)

## Craft Usage Tooltip

**Craft Usage Tooltip** is a lightweight World of Warcraft addon that displays which professions can use an ingredient directly in the item tooltip.

## Feet of Azeroth

A small travel companion for Azeroth. Display your current coordinates and track the distance you've travelled through the world of Warcraft.

## Gasp of Pandaria

**Gasp of Pandaria** is a light and relaxing mini-game inspired by the calm aesthetics of Pandaria. It features a simple 4×4 puzzle grid designed for quick, enjoyable sessions between quests, dungeons, or raids.

## Traveler's Notebook

While playing World of Warcraft, I often keep a real notebook next to me to write down useful information: NPC locations, quests, ideas, and things I don't want to forget. **Traveler's Notebook** brings this habit directly into the game.

## Wisdom of Azeroth

**Wisdom of Azeroth** is a lightweight World of Warcraft flavor addon that displays a random Azeroth-themed proverb when you log in.
